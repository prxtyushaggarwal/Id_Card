Key university regulations governing RFID security, fine structures, and document verification.

Instant Security Lockdown

  • Turnstile Deactivation: Former RFID UID is blacklisted across campus gates within 2 hours of report.
  • Library & Hostels: Prevents unauthorized entry to Uni-Mall, Central Library, and hostel turnstiles.
  • Auto Sync: Hostel mess and attendance bio-punches sync to your new card automatically.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? Read Official Answers

Clear guidance for students regarding ID card loss, fees, and campus protocol.

How do I obtain a Police Report for a lost ID card?

If you misplaced your card outside campus, you can generate an instant e-Lost Article Report on the Punjab Police Saanjh portal. If misplaced inside the campus premises, report to the Security & Proctor Office in Block 29 to receive an official lost certificate slip.

Will my hostel gate punch and mess entry work on the new card?

Yes. Once your replacement smart card is encoded in Block 30 Room 102, the new RFID UID automatically syncs with central hostel database servers within 3 hours. Your hostel turnstiles and mess meal allowance will seamlessly activate.

What if my ID card is cracked, chipped, or barcode faded?

Select the Damaged or Defaced category on Step 1. You do not need a police report. Simply upload a photo of the damaged card. Note that you must physically surrender the damaged card at Block 32 Counter 4 when collecting your new one.

Can my roommate or friend collect my card with my fee receipt?

No. Due to university security regulations, student identity cards are sensitive credentials. Physical collection requires the student's own biometric verification or physical signature at DSW Counter 4.

I have end-term exams tomorrow morning. How can I get an urgent card?

Choose the Urgent (24-Hour Express Print) option at Step 3. Your card request gets highest priority in the Block 30 queue and is dispatched to DSW Counter 4 within 24 working hours. Meanwhile, print your official fee receipt to carry as temporary exam clearance.
